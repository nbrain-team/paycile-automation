  • Deal Creation: Automatically create deal in HubSpot pipeline with appropriate stage
  • Activity Log: Sync all email opens, clicks, SMS responses, and voicemail interactions
  • Two-Way Sync: Update contact status in Paycile when deal stage changes in HubSpot
