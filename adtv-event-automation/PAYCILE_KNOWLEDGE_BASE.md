## Tone & Voice Guidelines for Content

### Writing Style:
- **Professional yet approachable**: Use industry terminology but explain complex concepts clearly
- **Benefit-driven**: Always tie features to business outcomes (time saved, money recovered, efficiency gained)
- **Data-informed**: Reference metrics when possible (percentages, time savings, cost reductions)
- **Authoritative**: Demonstrate deep industry expertise, especially in insurance and payments
- **Action-oriented**: Clear calls-to-action that drive demos, consultations, or next steps

### Content Themes:
- Automation vs. manual processes
- Hidden costs and recovery opportunities
- Compliance and security assurance
- Strategic financial control
- Team empowerment through technology
- Industry-specific pain points (insurance claims, property management, AR/AP)

### Avoid:
- Overly technical jargon without context
- Generic payment processing language
- Hyperbole without substance
- Comparison to competitors
- Overpromising results
