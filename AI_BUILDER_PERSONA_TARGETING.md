## 🎯 Best Practices

### 1. Match Persona to Audience
**Good:**
- Persona: CFO → Target: Insurance companies $50M-$500M revenue
- Persona: Property Finance → Target: Multi-property management firms

**Bad:**
- Persona: CFO → Target: Small businesses (use "Small Business Owner" instead)
- Persona: Accountant → Target: C-suite (use "CFO" or "Controller" instead)

### 2. Use Landing Pages Strategically
**When to Use:**
- Long-form educational campaigns
- High-value prospects (CFO, Controller)
- Multi-touch sequences (5+ steps)

**When to Skip:**
- Quick outreach (2-3 touches)
- Event invitations (use event URL instead)
- Re-engagement campaigns (direct to demo scheduling)

### 3. Leverage Persona-Specific Hooks
The AI has built-in messaging hooks for each persona. Let it use them:
- CFOs respond to: "Real-time visibility", "Strategic control", "Audit-ready"
- Controllers respond to: "96 days saved", "Team efficiency", "Workload reduction"
- AR/AP responds to: "Lost revenue", "Write-off reduction", "Automation"

### 4. Combine Personas for Complex Campaigns
Some campaigns target multiple personas:
```
Primary Persona: CFO
Secondary Mention: "Share with your Controller to see workflow benefits"
```

## 🚀 Using the Enhanced Builder

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Navigate to Builder** (`/builder`)

2. **Select Target Persona** (Required)
   - Choose from 8 strategic personas
   - AI tailors all messaging to this persona

3. **Select Landing Page Template** (Optional)
   - Choose matching landing page
   - Or select "None" for campaign-only

4. **Describe Campaign**
   - Be specific about goals
   - Mention pain points you want to emphasize
   - Example: "Re-engage insurance CFOs, focus on audit-ready automation"

5. **Configure Channels & Steps**
   - Select communication channels
   - Set number of steps (2-15)
   - Choose tone and industry

6. **Generate**
   - AI creates persona-specific campaign
   - Reviews content with quantifiable ROI
   - Refine any step as needed

7. **Save to Templates**
   - One-click save
   - Ready for live campaigns
