### 4. WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN AI RESPONSES

**Newbury Voice Characteristics:**

✅ **Direct, no-fluff** 
   - Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
   - Gets to the point

✅ **Specific numbers**
   - "$84K/month", "80 hours/month", "18%"
   - Never vague claims

✅ **Signature phrases**
   - "I live for helping leaders..."
   - "They didn't need more people. They needed to see what was stuck."
   - "Full transparency: ..."

✅ **Problem → Clarity → Action structure**
   - Identifies pain
   - Shows solution
   - Clear next step

✅ **Katie's sign-off**
   - Always ends with "-Katie"
   - Often includes P.S. for urgency

❌ **Should NEVER see:**
   - Corporate jargon
   - Emojis
   - Long paragraphs
   - Pushy sales language
   - Vague promises
